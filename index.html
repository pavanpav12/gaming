Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account and start playing?
Click on "Sign In" or "Play Free" on the top right, fill in your email, verify, and you're ready to explore thousands of games. It takes less than 60 seconds!
Are there any hidden fees or subscriptions?
Stackly is completely free to join. You can purchase games individually or subscribe to Stackly+ for extra perks, but the core platform (free games, tournaments, community) is 100% free.
How do tournaments work?
We run daily, weekly, and monthly tournaments across many games. Register through the Tournament Hub, compete in brackets, and earn cash prizes, badges, and exclusive skins.
What platforms does Stackly support?
PC (Windows/macOS), PlayStation, Xbox, and cloud gaming via mobile browser. Most games are cross-platform.
How do I refund a purchase?
You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase if you've played less than 2 hours. Visit your account settings → Order History.
